# Dehydration and ORS

Dehydration happens when the body loses more fluid than it takes in, usually from vomiting, loose motions, heavy sweating or fever.

## Signs of dehydration
- Feeling very thirsty, dry mouth and lips.
- Passing little or dark yellow urine.
- Tiredness, dizziness or light-headedness.
- Sunken eyes in more serious cases.

## What ORS is
ORS (oral rehydration salts) is a World Health Organization formula of salts and sugar that restores fluid balance. It is available at any pharmacy without prescription.

## How to use ORS
- Dissolve one sachet in the volume of clean water written on the packet, usually 1 litre.
- Sip slowly over the next few hours. Do not boil the solution after mixing.
- Use within 24 hours of mixing.
- Continue normal food and breast-feeding for children.

## When to see a doctor
- Vomiting or loose motions lasting more than 2 days.
- Blood in stool, high fever, or no urine for 8 hours.
- Infants, elderly people and those with kidney disease dehydrate faster: see a doctor early.

## Emergency signs
Confusion, fainting, rapid weak pulse, or cold clammy skin: seek emergency care immediately.
