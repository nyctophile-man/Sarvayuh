# Fever basics

A fever is a body temperature above 100.4 F (38 C). Most fevers in adults are caused by viral infections and settle in 2 to 3 days.

## What helps at home
- Drink plenty of fluids: water, soup, coconut water or ORS.
- Rest as much as possible.
- Wear light clothing and keep the room comfortably cool.
- A lukewarm sponge can help if the fever feels very uncomfortable.

## Paracetamol for fever
Paracetamol is commonly used for fever. The usual adult dose written on the pack should be followed, and never more than 4 grams in a day. Avoid alcohol while taking it. If you take other medicines, ask a pharmacist first.

## When to see a doctor
- Fever above 103 F (39.4 C) or fever lasting more than 3 days.
- Severe headache, stiff neck, rash, repeated vomiting or confusion.
- You are pregnant, over 65, or have diabetes, heart or kidney disease.

## Emergency signs
Trouble breathing, chest pain, seizures, fainting, or inability to keep fluids down: seek emergency care immediately.
